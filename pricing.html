Frequently asked questions

Can I use OpenRead for free?

Yes. The core reader, local notes, highlights, search, and export workflow are free.

What happens when I upgrade?

Paid plans add more AI explanation and translation capacity while keeping your notes local.

Do my notes leave my browser?

No. Saved notes and highlights stay in browser storage unless you export or share them yourself.

How does custom pricing work?

Contact us with your deployment needs and we will scope limits, support, and billing.
